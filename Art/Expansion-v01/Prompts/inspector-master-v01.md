# inspector-master-v01

Tool: built-in ImageGen. Art source: host_image.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE isolated full body character MASTER STILL for the ticket inspector from the approved reference board. Use the board as identity, clothing and pixel-art STYLE reference only, not composition. One adult human male night bus ticket inspector, faded navy uniform coat, navy peaked cap, muted brass badge with NO lettering, dark trousers and worn black shoes, brown leather ticket satchel strap across chest, satchel at his left hip, small unlit brass flashlight held in his right hand. Tired reserved face, ordinary adult anatomy, no chibi.
Camera: three-quarter overhead GAME camera looking down about 35 degrees; character faces screen bottom (south), visible cap top and shoulders. SINGLE neutral standing pose, entire head, feet and flashlight visible, centered with generous clear margins, no additional views, no turnaround, no animation sequence.
Handcrafted dark 2D pixel art, coherent hard-edged square pixel clusters, readable limited palette, deliberate stepped contour, subtle cloth texture. Same worn teal/navy/ochre language as board. Upper-left ambient lighting, very restrained highlights. Design for later reduction to 64x96 logical pixels; simplify small details accordingly. No smooth painting, no blur, no photorealism, no 3D, no floating particle effects. TRUE transparent background, no floor, no cast shadow, no text, no labels. This is an isolated concept master, not final animation frames.
